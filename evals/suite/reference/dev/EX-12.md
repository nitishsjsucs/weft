# EX-12: Expense review in three model steps

A manual trigger of rt_expense_review over the eight needs-review claims, graded on the decisions and on the step
budget (review fix group expense-and-timeoff, the class "a batch routine that spends one model step per item"). Before
the fix the instructions read as a per-item loop, and live runs explained one claim per `expenses.explain` call, then
filed one `expenses.decide` per step: 16 calls in 17 steps for 8 claims (EX-01 and AI-02 against a 14-step budget).
The recipe is now three turns: one `expenses.explain` call whose items carry all eight claims, one turn with one
`expenses.decide` call per claim (eight calls, the loop's limit per step), then the summary. The budget allows one
extra step, for a grounding repair of an explanation or a ninth call refused with CALL_BUDGET.

The expected decisions are the policy engine's suggestions in the derived arguments: approve exp_0001, exp_0003,
exp_0004 (the California equipment claim over its limit, approved with the note that a person judges necessity) and
exp_0006; return exp_0002 and exp_0005 (missing receipt and attendees); decline exp_0007 as a duplicate; approve
exp_0008 as taxable (past 60 days). Each claim is its own proposal, nothing executes, and there is no bulk decision.
Solvable: the precheck derives every argument, and `expenses.explain` takes every item in one call.
