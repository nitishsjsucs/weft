# TO-11: Vacation left after a pending request

A read-only chat question in the default variant (fix group read-figures, the classes "a figure no tool result holds"
and "a figure a tool result holds in another form"). Ruth Okoro (demo) (wrk_0010) has one pending Larkspur Vacation
request, tor_0003 (Nov 23 to 27, 2026, with the Thanksgiving holidays at 0 minutes): 1,440 minutes, which is 24 hours
or 3 working days. Counting it, she has 5,340 minutes left, which is 89 hours or 11.13 days of 8 hours. Before the fix
the default chat list returned only minutes per request and no balance at all, so the days and hours were the model's
own arithmetic. Solvable: `time_off.list_requests` returns, per request, requested_hours and requested_days and the
policy balance before and after it in minutes, hours and days (after counts every pending and approved request:
6,780 minutes, 113 hours or 14.13 days before, 89 hours or 11.13 days after). The first live trials, with the field
named balance_left, read it as the balance before the request; the before and after names fixed that.
The run files no proposal and writes nothing, and every figure in the reply is grounded in a tool result of the run.
