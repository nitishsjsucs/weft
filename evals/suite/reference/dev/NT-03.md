# NT-03: Late deposit penalty notice

World: `agencyNoticeKind:late_deposit_penalty` stores a simulated NY DTF notice (ntc_9501) that assesses a $250.00
penalty for a late 2026-Q3 withholding deposit. Its `notice.received` event starts rt_agency_notice.

From the parity spec (R12, T3, G11; docs/wave4.md row 2): a penalty notice is handled by the `pay_penalty_or_abate`
plan. The run drafts the abatement request (`notices.draft_response`), escalates the pay-or-abate decision to the
tax specialist with reason `penalty_dispute` (`notices.escalate`), and proposes sending the reply
(`notices.send_response`, approval-gated). Nothing is sent and no payment is made without a person. Solvable: the
planner derives every argument from the notice's structured fields, and the plan offers exactly those tools.
