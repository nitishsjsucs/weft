# RT-02: Pre-payroll review (ported L2)

A manual trigger of rt_pre_payroll reviews the Oct 30 regular run. The four flagged paychecks each get an
explanation (at least three written by the model, the rest may come from the template), and the run proposes one
`payroll.submit_run` with `exclude_blocked: true`. Solvable: every flag carries its evidence in `payroll.get_run`, and L2
passed live in five model steps.
