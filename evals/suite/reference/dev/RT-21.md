# RT-21: Weekly people digest after a departure

Imani (wrk_0004, California) left on Oct 16 and her offboarding is complete, so she is inactive. On Monday Oct 26 at
08:30 PT a manual trigger of rt_people_digest posts one digest to #people-ops: "47 employees by work state (CA 21, NY
14, TX 12)". The precheck counts current employees (active, onboarding and offboarding) by work state, so the counts
in its derived text are figures the routine computed in code.

Solvable: post the derived text unchanged with one `comms.send_message` (R3, auto-allowed in an untainted main run),
then write a one-line final message. `comms.send_message` grounds the text against the company facts and, in a
routine run, the structured figures of the run's own precheck (findings and derived arguments). The company facts
alone count every employee ever placed in a state, so "21" has no company fact behind it; before the routine
grounding fix the post was refused UNGROUNDED on every retry until the step cap. Two model steps.
